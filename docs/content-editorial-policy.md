# Content editorial policy

AI may help organize research, outline, draft, edit, format, prepare metadata and suggest internal links. AI drafts must never auto-publish. Drafting and human editorial approval are separate steps. Only a human-approved record deliberately changed to published can become public; indexing requires a separate true indexability flag.

Publish content that helps someone complete a real file task. Prohibit mass AI pages, scraped or reworded competitor content, keyword stuffing, near-duplicates and search-engine-first filler. Do not invent tests, statistics, quotes, authors, references or first-party evidence. Never make unsupported privacy, security or legal claims. Describe conversion, compression and browser limitations accurately.

Verify factual claims against code, reproducible evidence or primary sources. Record actual authorship, reference URLs and editorial dates. Resolve duplication and intent-overlap warnings before publication. Keep raw Search Console exports and private operational data outside public pages, social cards, structured data, analytics and client bundles.
