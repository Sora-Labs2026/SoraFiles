Installing Desktop

Download the installer for your operating system from Downloads. Verify the published SHA-256 checksum before opening it. Desktop does not update itself automatically.

Windows SmartScreen

The current Windows installer is distributed unsigned, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may say that Windows protected your PC. After verifying the SHA-256 checksum and confirming that you downloaded the installer from the SoraFiles release page, select More info, then Run anyway. A publisher warning may continue until SoraFiles ships a certificate-signed installer.

Opening SoraFiles on macOS

The current Mac builds are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, use these steps for a SoraFiles download you trust:

  1. 1Open the DMG, drag SoraFiles Desktop to Applications, then try opening it once.
  2. 2Open System Settings, select Privacy & Security, and scroll to the message about SoraFiles Desktop.
  3. 3Select Open Anyway, then confirm Open when prompted.

This approves the app on this Mac; it does not notarize it. If macOS reports that the app is damaged or will harm your computer, do not override that alert. See Apple’s opening instructions.

Trial and activation

Your free 7-day trial begins automatically when you first open SoraFiles Desktop on Windows, macOS or Linux. To continue after the trial, choose License → Activate License and paste the key from your purchase email. Works offline after setup; file processing stays on your device.

Can't find your license email?

Check the inbox for the email address used at checkout, including Spam or Junk. Look for “Your License Key is Ready” from noreply@dodopayments.com. If it landed in Spam, mark it as not spam. If you still cannot find it, contact us for help.

Replacing a device

Open License → Replace device. SoraFiles sends a short-lived code to the purchaser email on the verified order. The address is masked in Desktop. After verification, the one-time replacement checkout opens; payment alone cannot bypass email verification.
